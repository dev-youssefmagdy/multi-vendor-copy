Getting Started

Creating an account is simple. Click the "Sign Up" or "Get Started" button on any page, choose your plan (you can start for free), provide your email address and set a password. You'll receive a confirmation email and can start building your store right away.
Not at all! Ecommet is designed to be beginner-friendly. Our drag-and-drop store builder, pre-built templates, and intuitive dashboard mean you can launch a professional store without writing a single line of code.
Most merchants set up their store in under 30 minutes. Simply pick a template, add your products, configure payment and shipping settings, and you're ready to go. Our setup wizard guides you through every step.
Yes! Our Basic plan is completely free with no time limit and no credit card required. It includes up to 10 products, 100 orders per month, and a full set of core features to get you started.

Billing & Plans

Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan from your account settings at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. When downgrading, your current plan remains active until the end of the billing period.
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and various local payment methods depending on your region. All payments are processed securely via our payment partners.
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not satisfied within the first 14 days, contact our support team for a full refund. No questions asked.
Yearly billing saves you approximately 20% compared to monthly billing. For example, the Standard plan costs $19/month or $190/year — saving you $38 per year.

Store Management

Technical

Absolutely. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest. We conduct regular security audits and comply with industry standards. Your store and customer data are backed up daily.

We guarantee 99.9% uptime for all plans. Our infrastructure runs on redundant cloud servers with automatic failover to ensure your store is always available to your customers.
